# Personas

One product, several depths of engagement. The hive has to delight each of these people **without compromising the experience for the others**. That is the job of [[Design Principles#5. Depth on demand|Depth on demand]].

## The engagement dial
```
 Casual ─────────────── Hobbyist ─────────────── Obsessed
 "Keep it alive,        "Help me grow well,      "Give me every
  make it beautiful"     teach me as I go"        variable and the API"
```
The **Aesthete** sits across the dial. They care about the object first.

---

## 🌿 The Casual
**Who:** Busy, design-conscious and probably a renter. Loves the *idea* of plants and has a graveyard of former houseplants.
**Wants:** Greenery that looks great and survives without them thinking about it.
**Fears:** Mess, leaks, dead plants, another device that nags.
**Success looks like:** Months go by and the only thing they've done is top up water and enjoy compliments.
**The hive gives them:** Autopilot. Pre-planted or pre-selected cells, almost no decisions, rare and clear prompts.

## 🌱 The Hobbyist
**Who:** Already keeps plants and enjoys it. Might grow herbs, collect interesting species, or propagate cuttings.
**Wants:** To grow more and grow better, with variety and a sense of progress. Likes learning.
**Fears:** Being locked into a single crop or proprietary pods; losing the hands-on joy.
**Success looks like:** A hive full of plants they chose, thriving, and they understand *why*.
**The hive gives them:** Guided control. Plant-specific care profiles, insights ("this cell is drinking more than usual"), freedom to plant what they like.

## 🔬 The Obsessed
**Who:** Gardener-engineer. Tracks pH and EC, runs a home automation setup, may already have grow tents or hydroponics.
**Wants:** Data, fine control, custom recipes, integrations, experimentation.
**Fears:** Black boxes, cloud lock-in, products that dumb things down.
**Success looks like:** The hive is the centrepiece of their home automation and a platform for experiments they share with others.
**The hive gives them:** Full control. Per-cell schedules and recipes, raw sensor data, integrations, open interfaces.
*These are likely the earliest Kickstarter backers and evangelists.*

## 🖼️ The Aesthete
**Who:** Interior-design-led buyer. May not care about gardening at all. Buys it because it is the most striking thing they could put on that wall.
**Wants:** A living art piece that fits their space, palette and style.
**Fears:** It looking cheap, techy or messy; it looking dead.
**Success looks like:** The hive is the focal point of the room and a conversation starter.
**The hive gives them:** Composition. Finishes, curated plant palettes, layout design tools, a beautiful object even before the plants fill in.
