# Vision

> **A living wall that grows with you.**
> Modular planter cells that join together into a hive: a piece of art on your wall that quietly takes care of itself.

## The idea in one paragraph
The Hive is a wall-mounted garden made of **cells**. Each cell holds a plant in the right growing medium for that plant. Cells join together to form a **hive** that can start with a handful of cells and spread across a whole wall over time. When a new cell is added it becomes part of the hive straight away. It gets water, nutrients, light and attention from the hive's **Heart** and **Brain** with no plumbing, wiring or setup from the owner. The result works as a design piece first, the thing you notice when you walk into the room, and it is also a complete, self-regulating garden that suits someone who just wants green on the wall as well as someone who wants to tune every drop.

## Why this should exist
- **Plants make spaces feel alive, but keeping them alive is hard.** Most people have killed a houseplant. Inconsistent watering is the usual cause, not a lack of interest.
- **Living walls today are out of reach.** They are either professional installations (expensive, permanent, maintained by contractors) or static wall pockets that drip on the floor and are dead by the end of summer.
- **Smart gardens look like appliances.** Automated indoor gardens exist, but they are countertop gadgets. Nobody hangs them in the living room as a statement.
- **No product does all of these at once:** beautiful enough to *be* the art, simple enough to install yourself, smart enough to look after itself, and modular enough to start small and grow.

## What we believe
1. **The wall is a canvas and plants are the medium.** The hive is a composition, and the owner is its author.
2. **Care should be automatic and engagement optional.** The hive keeps plants alive on its own. Getting involved should always be rewarding and never required.
3. **Growth is the story,** both of the plants and of the hive itself. A hive should be visibly bigger and richer a year after you bought it.
4. **Technology should disappear.** Pumps, valves, sensors and computers serve the plants and should stay out of sight.

## Core concepts
| Concept | What it means to the owner |
|---|---|
| **Cell** | One unit of the hive, holding one planting. The building block. |
| **Hive** | The combined arrangement of cells on the wall, which behaves as one living system. |
| **Heart** | Circulates water and nutrients through the hive. |
| **Brain** | Senses, decides and coordinates. It knows every cell and what each needs. |
| **Joining** | Adding a cell is as easy as placing it. It connects itself into the hive's water, power and intelligence. |
| **Self-regulation** | The hive watches moisture, feeding and light and adjusts on its own. |

See [[Glossary]] for definitions and [[System Concept]] for how these behave.

## What success looks like
- Someone walks into a room and sees the hive as art **before** realising it's alive.
- A person who has killed every houseplant they've owned keeps a hive thriving for years.
- Adding a cell feels like snapping in a Lego brick: *click*, and it's part of the whole.
- No two hives look alike. Owners show theirs off, share layouts, and plan their next expansion.
- The obsessed tinker endlessly, and the casual owner forgets there's anything to tinker with.

## What it is / what it isn't
| It is | It isn't |
|---|---|
| A design object that happens to be a garden | A gadget that happens to hold plants |
| Modular and expandable by the owner | A fixed, installer-dependent living wall |
| Self-regulating and forgiving | A system that punishes you for going on holiday |
| Equally at home with succulents, herbs, ferns or moss | A single-crop hydroponic appliance |
| Calm and quiet | Noisy, blinking, or demanding attention |

## The horizon
These are possibilities that show where the idea could go. They are not commitments.
- **A family of cell types** beyond planters: light cells, reservoir cells, art or blank cells for composition, display cells, even water-feature cells.
- **Curated hives:** plant palettes and layouts designed by artists, designers and horticulturists.
- **Community:** shared hive layouts, growing recipes and plant pairings.
- **Living subscriptions:** seasonal swaps of pre-planted cell inserts.
- **Beyond the home:** cafés growing herbs on the wall, offices, hotels, retail.
