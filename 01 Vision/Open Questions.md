# Open Questions

Gaps to resolve. When answered, move the answer into the relevant note and log significant ones in the [[Decision Log]].

## Product identity
- [ ] **Name:** "The Hive" is a working name. What is the product called?
- [ ] **Geometry:** hexagon (true "hive"), square (like the EKET prototype), or something else? Is geometry fixed in the vision, or should the prototype stay agnostic?
- [ ] **Heart and Brain:** one combined cell or two separate cell types? Can a large hive have more than one Heart (e.g. for height or reach)?

## Water & plants
- [ ] **Water source:** self-contained reservoir that the owner tops up, or optional connection to mains or a drain? How often is it acceptable to top up (weekly, monthly)?
- [ ] **Run-off:** closed loop (excess water returns to the Heart) or no run-off (precise dosing)?
- [ ] **Growing medium:** soil-based substrates matched per plant (as described), semi-hydro (LECA, pon), or both?
- [ ] **Plants at purchase:** ship live plants, plant-ready inserts, or bring-your-own?
- [ ] **Edibles:** are herbs and greens a core use case or a niche?

## Light & environment
- [ ] **Lighting:** must the hive thrive on a windowless wall (integrated grow light), or is it designed for bright rooms with light as an add-on?
- [ ] **Ambient/decorative light:** is the hive glowing at night part of the vision?

## Experience & software
- [ ] **App:** required, optional, or none for the Casual owner? Should the hive work fully offline?
- [ ] **Smart-home integration:** important for launch (Obsessed persona), or later?
- [ ] **Status on the hive:** light cues, a small display, or nothing visible?

## Market & business
- [ ] **Primary launch persona:** which persona leads the Kickstarter story?
- [ ] **Positioning & price:** premium design object, or accessible entry point with premium options?
- [ ] **Launch geography:** which markets first? (This affects electrical certification, shipping live plants, and voltage.)
- [ ] **Home only, or commercial too** (cafés, offices) in early messaging?

## Team
- [ ] Who are the collaborators, and who owns which areas (vision, ID, engineering, horticulture, business)?
