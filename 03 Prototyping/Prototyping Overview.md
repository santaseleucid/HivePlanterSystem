# Prototyping Overview

> The vision is free of constraints. The prototypes are where we meet reality on purpose, **one question at a time.**

Prototypes exist to answer specific questions. Each one should say what it proves, what it doesn't, and how it informs the vision.

## Core questions the prototypes must answer
1. **Joining:** can a new cell connect to water, power and data simply by being placed, and be recognised automatically?
2. **Distribution:** can water and nutrients reach every cell reliably, whatever its position (height, distance from the Heart)?
3. **Sensing:** can each cell's moisture (and other conditions) be measured well enough to regulate care?
4. **Regulation:** can the Brain keep a mixed hive of plants healthy unattended for weeks?
5. **Safety:** can we guarantee no leaks or water damage, and fail safe when something goes wrong?
6. **Plants:** do plants actually thrive in the cells with the chosen substrates?
7. **Beauty:** does it look good enough on a wall to be the art?

## Prototype roadmap (proposed)
| Stage | Type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **v0** | Works-like, built on IKEA EKET | Prove the core loop cheaply: fluidics, sensing, control, joining concept. [[Prototype v0 - EKET]] |
