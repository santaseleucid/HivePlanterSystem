# Kickstarter Plan

## Purpose
Validate market appetite, and fund and de-risk the first production run if appetite is real.

## What the campaign needs
1. **A working prototype** proving the core loop: cells join, water flows, plants thrive, the hive regulates itself. See [[Prototyping Overview]].
2. **A picture of the finished product:** renders, film and the [[Aesthetic Vision]], so backers can see what they're buying into. See [[Design Overview]].
3. **A story:** why this should exist ([[Vision]]), told through the people it's for ([[Personas]]).
4. **Clear reward tiers:** starter hive, expansion packs, early-bird, maybe a maker or developer tier.
5. **Credible plan & risks:** timeline, manufacturing approach, what could go wrong.

> ⚠️ Check Kickstarter's current rules for hardware projects (e.g. requirements to show a working prototype and how renders/simulations may be presented) before planning the visual assets.

## Pre-launch validation (ideas)
- [ ] Landing page + email sign-up; track conversion by message and audience
- [ ] Social content showing the prototype and the vision
- [ ] Customer interviews per persona
- [ ] Price testing for tiers

## Success criteria
*To define:* funding goal, sign-ups before launch, target conversion, number of add-on cells per backer.
