# Market Landscape

Adjacent categories and examples to research. *Verify all of these. This is a starting list, not analysis.*

| Category | Examples to research | What they do well | Where they fall short (for us) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Smart indoor gardens | Click & Grow, AeroGarden, Rise Gardens, Lettuce Grow | Automation, easy growing | Appliance aesthetic, countertop/floor, crop-focused |
| Commercial living walls | LiveWall, Florafelt, various green-wall installers | Scale, impact | Professional install, expensive, not modular for consumers |
| Consumer wall planters | Felt pocket planters, wall-mounted pots | Cheap, simple | Manual watering, drips, plants die |
| Preserved moss / plant art | Moss walls, framed preserved plants | Pure aesthetic | Not alive, no growth |
| Modular wall décor | Hexagonal shelves, modular acoustic panels | Composable, personal | No plants, no intelligence |

## Our position (hypothesis)
The intersection of **wall art**, **modular**, and **self-regulating**. Each neighbour above has one or two of these, and none has all three.
