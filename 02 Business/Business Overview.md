# Business Overview

## Near-term goal
Find out whether there is **real market appetite** for a modular, self-regulating living wall, using a [[Kickstarter Plan|Kickstarter campaign]] backed by a working prototype and a compelling picture of the finished product.

## Hypotheses to validate
| # | Hypothesis | How we'd know |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | People want living walls at home but are put off by cost, installation and upkeep | Interviews, landing-page sign-ups, comments |
| H2 | "Art first" positioning attracts buyers beyond gardeners | Conversion of design-led audiences vs plant audiences |
| H3 | Modularity drives repeat purchases (owners add cells over time) | Stated intent; add-on pledge uptake |
| H4 | Self-regulation is the key purchase driver for Casual buyers | Message testing |
| H5 | Enthusiasts (the Obsessed) will back early and evangelise | Early-bird uptake, community engagement |
| H6 | There is a viable price point for a starter hive | Pledge-level distribution |

## Revenue model (ideas)
- **Starter hive:** Heart, Brain and a few planter cells
- **Expansion cells:** the long-tail, repeat purchase
- **Consumables:** nutrients, substrates, replacement inserts
- **Plants / living subscriptions:** seasonal pre-planted inserts
- **Premium finishes and curated designs**
- **Commercial / B2B** (later)

## Key risks
- Hardware is hard: cost, manufacturing, certification (water + electricity in homes)
- Water damage liability and consumer trust
- Shipping live plants across borders
- Price point vs perceived value
- A design product that only photographs well in large configurations
