# Engineering Overview

Subsystems and key technical questions. Split each section into its own note as work deepens.

## Fluidics
Pump, solenoid valves, drippers, reservoir, nutrient dosing, drainage or recirculation.
- How do we deliver precise amounts to each cell regardless of height?
- Closed loop vs dose-to-zero run-off?
- How are nutrients mixed and dosed?

## Cell link (interconnect)
The physical, water, power and data connection between neighbouring cells.
- How does a cell seal water connections when it's added or removed?
- How does the Brain discover a new cell and its position?
- Bus or star topology for power and data?

## Sensing
Moisture per cell; possibly light, temperature, humidity, reservoir level, leak detection.

## Lighting
Grow light and ambient light; per cell or per hive.

## Control electronics & firmware
Brain hardware, valve and pump drivers, power supply, safety interlocks, connectivity.

## Software & app
Owner experience, care profiles, alerts, integrations, offline behaviour.

## Horticulture & substrates
Plant selection, substrate per plant type, nutrient regimes, root-space limits.

## Safety & compliance
Water + mains electricity in the home, low-voltage design, leak containment, certification for target markets.
