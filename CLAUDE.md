# Notes for Claude

- Follow the conventions in README.md: frontmatter (`status`, `tags`), `[[wikilinks]]`, templates in `Templates/`.
- Keep `01 Vision/` free of practical constraints (no specific parts, vendors, IKEA EKET, costs). Practical/prototype material goes in `03 Prototyping/` or `04 Engineering/`.
- Use the vocabulary in `01 Vision/Glossary.md` consistently (Cell, Hive, Heart, Brain, etc.).
- Add new unknowns to `01 Vision/Open Questions.md` rather than silently assuming.
